Rat Kills Scientist

When scientific progress goes too fast, unexpected results have extreme consequences

In a shocking series of events, an unnamed scientist was found dead at the bottom of a well on a seemingly deserted island.

Last week, investigators found a series of journals on a island that led to the discovery of a dead scientist. The scientist had been creating a rat with human-level intelligence and had recently fired his only employee. We believe that the rat that he had created caused his death by trapping him in well on the island.

The journals detailed his dissmissal of Tayloe, his employee, and revealed clues about what had happened on the island. The September 11th journal reads:

"In order to facilitate my descent into the well I knotted a length of three-quarter-inch rope at one-foot intervals to make a rude ladder. I reached the bottom easily enough, but after only a few minutes of groping for the key, my flashlight gave out and I returned to the surface. A few feet from the top I heard excited squeaks from Barney, and upon obtaining ground level I observed that the rope was almost completely severed. Apparently it had chafed against the edge of the masonry and the little fellow perceiving my plight had been doing his utmost to warn me.
I have now replaced that section of rope and arranged some old sacking beneath it to prevent a recurrence of the accident. I have replenished the batteries in my flashlight and am now prepared for the final descent. These few moments I have taken off to give myself a breathing spell and to bring my journal up to date. Perhaps I should fix myself a sandwich as I may be down there longer than seems likely at the moment."
The investigators have deduced that the rat, know as "Barney", chewed through the rope the first time the scientist went into the well and the had not chafed like the scientist thought. It is likely that Barney ultimately completed his goal in the end. Enough clues were revealed in Journal entries that investigators feel confident that Barney was behind scientist's death.

This story is a reminder that scientific progress needs rules and regulations to stop it from becoming dangerous. This points to the growing problem of AI, and how it could be dangerous if not controled.
