How a Group of Schoolgirls Changed Social Norms at Their School

When students speak out, powerful change happens

After years of the same routines, one day a brave girl decided to speak out for change. Alma, a student in Miss Ralston's class helped end stereotypes and sexist social norms.

Changing a tradition takes patience, but the students showed that speaking up respectfully can make change possible. Their example reminds us that school culture is shaped by the people who are willing to improve it.
