Your Fears May Not Even be Threats in Reality

Imagination is a powerful thing, but it can also create unrealistic fears

Spiders and snakes are some of the most common fears, but if you really think about it, are they the most dangerous? Your brain can create fears from how creatures look or smell.

You'll often hear people scream, "Spider!" or, "Look out! There's a snake!" but in reality these animals are not that dangerous and are just fears created in our minds. For example, spiders are incredibly safe. Very few people are hurt or killed by spiders compared to car crashes. In fact, deaths caused by spiders are very rare and near zero per year. Snakes are another case of this, and while they are much more dangerous, snake bites only result in a few deaths per year in developed areas.

These fears come from the mind and imagination. Snakes and spiders are strange and creepy animals which may cause us to see them as something dangerous. Children often have more imaginary fears like the dark and monsters under the bed. They have very strong imaginations which cause their minds to create fears that aren't dangerous. Their minds can make games of floor is lava seem dangerous because the line between imagination and reality is much more blurry to them. Sometimes you have to be careful what stories you tell young children because they might just view them as real threats.
